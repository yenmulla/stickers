Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

TwitSticker is designed with privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use our service. We do not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information.

2. Processing of Media (Local Processing)

When you convert a Twitter/X video or GIF using our service:

  • The media is downloaded to your browser and all video processing and sticker generation happens locally on your machine using FFmpeg (via WebAssembly).
  • We do not upload your videos to our servers for conversion.
  • Because all processing happens on your device, we never retain, view, or distribute the media you process.

3. Analytics and Tracking

We use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic, understand user engagement, and optimize performance. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to collect aggregated, anonymous information (such as browser type, operating system, page views, and feature interactions). No personally identifiable information (PII) is shared with Google. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services by visiting Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Third-Party Services

TwitSticker interacts with Twitter/X APIs to fetch public media. Your use of the service to fetch media is subject to Twitter/X's public availability. We do not interact with your WhatsApp account; the stickers generated are downloaded to your device for you to manually import into WhatsApp.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact us.
