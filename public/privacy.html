1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to TwitSticker ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy governs your use of the TwitSticker website located at gifsticker.site and the official TwitSticker Android companion application (com.twitsticker.app).

TwitSticker is designed from the ground up to empower users to convert public videos, animated GIFs, and memes from Twitter/X into official 512x512 transparent animated WhatsApp stickers with minimum data exposure. By accessing our website or using our application, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Information We Collect (and Do Not Collect)

A. Personal Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not require user accounts. We never ask for, collect, store, or sell:

  • Names, usernames, home addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Passwords or login credentials.
  • Credit card or payment billing details (TwitSticker is 100% free).
  • Your WhatsApp chat messages, contacts, or personal profile data.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Information

When you visit our site or use our Android app, our infrastructure and trusted partners may automatically receive standard technical information:

  • Device & Browser Information: Browser type and version, device model, operating system, preferred language, and screen resolution.
  • Network & Request Data: Truncated/anonymized IP address (used strictly for geolocation estimation and security rate limiting), referring URL, and server request timestamps.
  • Usage Telemetry: Aggregated feature interactions, such as conversion clicks and error codes, to diagnose server performance.

3. Media Processing Architecture (Local & Ephemeral)

Our core architecture is intentionally engineered to minimize user media exposure:

Local Processing

When you convert videos via the web client, processing is performed inside your browser's local sandbox using WebAssembly (Wasm) FFmpeg. Video frames remain in your browser's private memory.

Ephemeral Cache

For server-assisted extracts or uploads, media files are stored only in temporary memory or ephemeral storage solely to complete the conversion, and are purged automatically via periodic cleanup cycles.

We do not index, search, catalog, or create galleries from your converted stickers.

4. Google Analytics & Google Tag Manager

We use Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager container GTM-N3CH5GSW) to understand aggregate user engagement and diagnose bugs.

  • Anonymized IP: IP addresses are masked by default, and personal identification is prohibited.
  • Aggregated Events: We measure conversion events (e.g., sticker generated, sticker downloaded, direct add to WhatsApp tapped) to improve user flows.
  • Opt-Out: You can prevent Google Analytics from recognizing you on return visits by disabling cookies in your browser or installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Android App Specific Disclosures (Google Play Compliance)

This section applies specifically to users of the TwitSticker Android Application (com.twitsticker.app) distributed via APK and Google Play.

A. Google Advertising ID (AD_ID Permission)

The Android app declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission. This identifier is used exclusively by certified advertising and analytics SDKs for fraud detection, ad measurement, and frequency capping. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads.

B. WhatsApp Sticker Content Provider

The app uses an internal Android ContentProvider (com.twitsticker.app.stickercontentprovider). This provider only serves sticker WebP files and metadata directly to the official WhatsApp package on your phone when you initiate the "Add to WhatsApp" action. It does not broadcast data to unauthorized third-party apps.

C. Device Permissions

The app requests only the permissions strictly necessary to perform its advertised functions: INTERNET (to resolve video links) and network state checks. We do not access contacts, camera, microphone, GPS location, or phone identity.

7. Third-Party Integrations (Twitter/X & WhatsApp)

TwitSticker interacts with external platforms only to provide the conversion service:

  • Twitter / X: When you provide a public tweet link, TwitSticker queries publicly available video endpoints to resolve the MP4 or GIF stream. We never require you to log into your Twitter account or authorize an OAuth application.
  • WhatsApp: WhatsApp is a trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc. TwitSticker is an independent tool that prepares compliant WebP stickers conforming to WhatsApp's published third-party sticker specification. We do not access, read, or transmit your WhatsApp chats or account credentials.

11. Data Retention & Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical safeguards to protect your experience:

  • Automatic Purging: Uploads and converted stickers are stored only ephemerally and wiped automatically to prevent data accumulation.
  • Restricted Access: Only automated scripts interact with temporary processing directories; human staff never inspect converted user media.
