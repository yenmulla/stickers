How do I convert a Twitter / X video to a WhatsApp sticker?

Simply copy the link of any tweet containing a video or GIF, paste it into the TwitSticker input bar, and click "Fetch Tweet". Adjust the start time and duration (up to 3.5s for maximum smoothness), then tap "Generate WhatsApp Sticker". Once ready, tap "ADD DIRECTLY TO WHATSAPP" to install it into your WhatsApp keyboard!

What are the strict WhatsApp animated sticker requirements?

WhatsApp enforces strict technical specs: exactly 512x512 pixels, WebP format with an alpha transparency channel, under 500 KB total file size, looping forever, and a duration $\le$ 10 seconds. TwitSticker automatically rescales, pads with transparency, and optimizes compression to guarantee 100% WhatsApp compliance.

Can I use TwitSticker on Android, iPhone, and WhatsApp Web?

Yes! On Android, you can use our 1-tap Direct WhatsApp export or download the companion APK. On iPhone and WhatsApp Web, you can download the raw .webp file or the .wastickers package and drag-and-drop or import via WhatsApp's native "+ Create" sticker tool.

Is TwitSticker free with no watermarks?

TwitSticker is 100% free and open-source. We do not inject any watermarks or brand logos into your animated stickers, and each sticker includes full custom WhatsApp EXIF metadata so you can set your own pack name and publisher author.
